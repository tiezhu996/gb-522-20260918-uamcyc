## Design Context

### Users

Fiber-route maintenance analysts and reviewers working at a desk with offline OTDR captures. They need to inspect dense traces, compare a capture with a baseline, review algorithm evidence, and record a defensible human conclusion without implying control of field equipment.

### Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, and trustworthy. The product should feel like a calibrated analysis instrument: calm under routine use, explicit about uncertainty, and rigorous when actions are irreversible.

### Aesthetic Direction

A light industrial analysis console with tinted neutral surfaces, strong accessible contrast, compact information density, and a vivid but limited signal palette. The OTDR trace is the primary visual signature. Avoid gradients, oversized headings, decorative cards, excessive rounding, generic dashboard tiles, and dark neon styling.

### Design Principles

1. Make the trace and its evidence the visual center of analysis workflows.
2. Keep repeated operational actions compact, predictable, and keyboard accessible.
3. Use color only for meaning; pair every status color with text or shape.
4. Preserve critical controls on narrow screens by reflowing instead of hiding them.
5. Expose uncertainty, permissions, and irreversible state transitions before confirmation.
